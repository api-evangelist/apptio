Artificial intelligence has become the headlining act in boardrooms. From fraud detection to personalised marketing and assistant tools for staff, it’s changing how companies compete. Yet a paradox runs through this progress: the smarter our systems become, the more complex it is to see what they cost – and whether they’re worth it.
