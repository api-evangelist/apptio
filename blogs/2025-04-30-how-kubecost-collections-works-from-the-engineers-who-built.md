Introduction Kubecost Collections provides teams with a powerful tool for analyzing costs in a way that flexibly represents each organization’s unique structure. Furthermore, it allows teams to bring Kubernetes and cloud costs together into a single, comprehensive view. The challenge with combining Kubernetes and cloud costs is that the two domains overlap considerably.
